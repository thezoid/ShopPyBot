<objective>
Establish the shared BF-03 login-verification foundation on the `RetailerPlugin` ABC: change the `login()` contract to return `bool`, add one concrete `_verify_login_generic` helper (D-11: one shared mechanism, not per-plugin duplication), and make `relaunch()` honor a failed re-login. This is the foundation every plugin `login()` conversion (plans 30-05, 30-06) builds on — those plans call `_verify_login_generic` and cannot land until this contract exists.

Purpose: A failed or ambiguous login can never again be reported successful (D-13), on any plugin.
Output: `login(self) -> bool` ABC, `_verify_login_generic`, a `relaunch()` return-value check, and the pre-fix `test_login_noop` rewritten.
</objective>
